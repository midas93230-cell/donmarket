Which books are actually alive

Polymarket shows you a price and a spread. It does not tell you whether anyone is on the other side. These 498 books were read directly from the CLOB and judged on four things: spread, volume, depth on both sides, and the minimum ticket you would have to commit.

25 August 2026, 22:33 UTCPublic endpoints, no API keyRead-only

The rule this page exists to make visible. Measured across six markets at the same instant: wherever there is volume, the spread is one tick; the only wide spread sat on the market nobody trades. A wide spread is not an opportunity waiting to be spotted — it is the price of having no counterparty. Sorting by spread finds traps, not edges.
