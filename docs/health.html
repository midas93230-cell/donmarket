Which books are actually alive

Polymarket shows you a price and a spread. It does not tell you whether anyone is on the other side. These 298 books were read directly from the CLOB and judged on four things: spread, volume, depth on both sides, and the minimum ticket you would have to commit.

29 August 2026, 22:04 UTCPublic endpoints, no API keyRead-only

The rule this page exists to make visible, and its limit. Measured today on books above $50k a day: where the tick is 0.001, 32 of 48 live books (66 %) sit at exactly one tick (widest: 270); where the tick is 0.01, 30 of 36 live books (83 %) sit at exactly one tick (widest: 30). Reading the widest of them live explains the gap: a bid at 0.999 and no ask at all — matches in progress or already decided. So a wide spread still means no counterparty. It just does not always mean nobody trades it. Sorting by spread finds traps, not edges.

These verdicts are enforced, not just published

The same measurement runs inside DONmarket, a page that connects your wallet and re-reads the book before anything is sent — then refuses the order and says why: a dead or trapped book, a size at exactly the minimum (any partial fill strands a remainder you cannot sell), a sell posted above the best ask, an order that crosses the spread when you did not mean to. Nothing to install, no account, and no key ever leaves your wallet.
