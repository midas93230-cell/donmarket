Which books are actually alive

Polymarket shows you a price and a spread. It does not tell you whether anyone is on the other side. These 294 books were read directly from the CLOB and judged on four things: spread, volume, depth on both sides, and the minimum ticket you would have to commit.

28 August 2026, 19:34 UTCPublic endpoints, no API keyRead-only

The rule this page exists to make visible. Measured across six markets at the same instant: wherever there is volume, the spread is one tick; the only wide spread sat on the market nobody trades. A wide spread is not an opportunity waiting to be spotted — it is the price of having no counterparty. Sorting by spread finds traps, not edges.

These verdicts are enforced, not just published

The same measurement runs inside DONmarket, a page that connects your wallet and re-reads the book before anything is sent — then refuses the order and says why: a dead or trapped book, a size at exactly the minimum (any partial fill strands a remainder you cannot sell), a sell posted above the best ask, an order that crosses the spread when you did not mean to. Nothing to install, no account, and no key ever leaves your wallet.
